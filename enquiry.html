Volunteer Roles

Food Packing

Pack food parcels for distribution to families in need.

When: Monday & Wednesday, 11am – 2pm

When: Flexible hours available

Admin Support

Assist with office tasks, coordination, and record-keeping.

When: Weekdays, 9am – 5pm
