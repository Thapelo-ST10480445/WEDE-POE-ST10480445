About Haven Community Food Bank

Nourishing our neighbours since 2015

Our Story

Haven Community Food Bank was founded in 2015 in Cape Town by a collective of five concerned residents who recognised the growing food insecurity in their community. What began as a small weekend soup kitchen operating from a garage in Khayelitsha has grown into a formal, registered non-profit organisation (NPO Reg: 2023/123456/08) with a dedicated warehouse in Philippi and satellite distribution points in Mitchells Plain and Strandfontein.

Today, Haven serves over 400 families monthly through a network of community distribution points, providing nutritious food and holistic support to under-resourced communities.

2026

Mission

To nourish our neighbours with dignity by providing reliable access to nutritious food, fostering community connections, and empowering individuals towards self-sufficiency.

Vision

A community where no one goes to bed hungry, and everyone has the opportunity to thrive.
