Who We Help

Eligibility

Haven exists for anyone in our community facing food insecurity. There's no complicated application process — just come to a distribution point during open hours.

Bring ID

A South African ID or any form of identification for the household member collecting

Proof of Address

Any recent document showing where you live (a utility bill, lease, or letter is fine)

Household Size

Let our team know how many people are in your household so parcels are sized fairly

No Judgement

First-time visitor or long-time neighbour — everyone is welcomed with dignity

What to Expect

How a Distribution Day Works

1

Arrive and Check In

Bring your ID and proof of address. A friendly volunteer will welcome you and note your household size.

2

We Pack Your Parcel

Volunteers pack a parcel of staples, tinned goods and fresh produce suited to your household.

3

Collect and Go Home

Pick up your parcel, ask us about other support, and head home with food for the week.
