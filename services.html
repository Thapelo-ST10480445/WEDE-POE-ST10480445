Who We Help

Eligibility

Haven exists for anyone in our community facing food insecurity. There's no complicated application process — just come to a distribution point during open hours.

Bring ID

A South African ID or any form of identification for the household member collecting

Proof of Address

Any recent document showing where you live (a utility bill, lease, or letter is fine)

Household Size

Let our team know how many people are in your household so parcels are sized fairly

No Judgement

First-time visitor or long-time neighbour — everyone is welcomed with dignity

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book in advance?

No booking is needed. Simply arrive at any distribution point during its open hours — see the schedule above.

How often can I collect a food parcel?

Households can collect one parcel per week from any single location during that location's open days.

What should I bring?

Please bring your ID (or the collecting household member's ID) and, if you have it, a proof of address. We'll help even without paperwork — just ask a volunteer.

Can I collect on behalf of someone else?

Yes. Bring your own ID along with the name of the household you're collecting for, and our team will assist you.

Is there support beyond food parcels?

Our Philippi hub also serves hot meals and can connect you with partner organisations for additional social support.
